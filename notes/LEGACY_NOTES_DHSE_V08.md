# Legacy note — Origins-DNA v0.8 DHSE bridge

The v0.8 direction was proposed after DHSE-001 established an exact reciprocal
forcing geometry around the positive self-dual coordinate.  The bridge is not
based on visual analogy: Watson--Crick complementation exactly inverts the
preregistered `A:T`, `C:G`, `R:Y` and `M:K` odds coordinates.

The project distinguishes three possible outcomes:

1. full DHSE improves over the projective-only ablation and survives separate nulls;
2. projective balances help but the DHSE nonlinear layer does not;
3. neither survives controls, leaving a correct symmetry without predictive value on the frozen task.

This note is append-only context. It does not record a result.
