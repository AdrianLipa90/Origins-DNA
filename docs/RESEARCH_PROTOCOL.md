# Origins-DNA research protocol v0.1

## Purpose

The project will test whether a proposed information-theoretic, NOEMA-inspired,
orbital/atomic representation of DNA provides measurable predictive value beyond
standard sequence representations.

This document defines validation gates. It does not assert a biological mechanism.

## Baseline question

For a pre-declared prediction task, does the proposed representation improve
held-out performance, calibration or sample efficiency over conventional controls?

## Required comparisons

1. Raw one-hot or 2-bit sequence representation.
2. k-mer frequency baselines.
3. A matched-capacity conventional model.
4. Shuffled-sequence and label-permutation null models.
5. Ablations removing each proposed orbital/atomic component.
6. Seed repetitions with confidence intervals.

## Initial candidate tasks

- predict synthetic mutation labels generated by a known process;
- classify sequence families on a public benchmark;
- predict a pre-declared regulatory or structural label from a curated dataset.

Public biological datasets will be added only with explicit provenance, licence,
version and checksum records.

## Promotion gates

A result may be described as a software result when tests and reproducibility checks pass.
A biological claim additionally requires:

- out-of-sample improvement against matched controls;
- robustness across seeds and data splits;
- failure of relevant null models;
- ablation evidence;
- a dimensionally and biologically interpretable mechanism;
- independent reproduction.

## Prohibited inference

Successful reversible encoding, compression, simulation or internal coherence must
not be described as evidence that DNA has been decrypted.
